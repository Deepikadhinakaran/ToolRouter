Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued
monthly at 1.5 days/month. Sick leave is capped at 10 days per year and does not
require prior approval, though a medical certificate is required for absences
longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused annual leave of up to 5 days can be
carried forward to the next year; anything beyond that is forfeited. Leave
requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance, except in emergencies.
